About the hotel

Built on quiet hospitality

Golden Royale Hotel opened its doors in 2010 with a simple idea: luxury should feel personal, not performative. Our 120 rooms and suites sit within walking distance of Enugu's business district and coastline, cared for by a team that remembers your name by the second visit.

Every stay includes access to our rooftop pool, in-house restaurant, and a concierge team available around the clock.
